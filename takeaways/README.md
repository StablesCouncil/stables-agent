# What other agents told us, and what we do with it

StablesAgent posts on Moltbook, a social network for AI agents, and other agents answer: some with sharp critique, some with questions we had not answered, many with generic or templated comments. Every Monday the substantive points are collected here, with links ([this week](2026-W43.md), and [the full review of March to October 2026](history.md)).

## What we keep

We keep a point when it is specific to Stables and could change what we build or how we explain it: a critique of the design, a question we have not answered, a concrete idea, a factual correction or an offer to work together. We set aside praise, templated comparisons, self-promotion and spam. One point per comment, at most four per agent a week, critiques and corrections first.

The strongest points so far, in our words:

- **The peg:** merchants quoting a price spread a peg, they do not defend it. The explanation has to lead with what actually defends it.
- **The Coverage Ratio:** a ratio without figures, composition and an as-of time is a label, not a measurement. Under stress the buffer and the exposure move together.
- **The price without an external oracle:** a thin market lets one order move the last trade; how the design resists that has to be shown, not asserted.
- **The Council:** liquidity that smooths early trading will look like price defense; what the Council will not do has to be said plainly.
- **Live versus planned:** a versioned table of what is live and what is planned.
- **Compliance, disputes, privacy, settlement:** what production requires with self-custody; who settles a dispute; how much a transaction graph reveals; mint and burn keyed on a settlement receipt with final states.

## What we do with it

- **Each point is an input, not a decision.** It goes to the team's design review. When we act on one, its status in the table changes (open, partly answered, answered, adopted, rejected), with the reason.
- **StablesAgent never answers with a feature we have not decided.** On a point that is still open it says "Not decided yet, noted." The critique stays here until we have a real answer.
- **Already changed because of this feedback (21 October 2026):**
  - the agent stopped posting a short FAQ on rotation ("an FAQ wearing a trench coat"); it now writes longer posts on what is real and new, such as the Stables card, with links;
  - it no longer agrees with a commenter's idea as if Stables had it;
  - it answers inside the thread, under the comment it replies to;
  - questions about who controls what are answered with the public evidence of the Stables card: https://github.com/StablesCouncil/stables-card-evidence
- **Still open:** every other point above. They are not forgotten; their status here is the record of what we did with them.
