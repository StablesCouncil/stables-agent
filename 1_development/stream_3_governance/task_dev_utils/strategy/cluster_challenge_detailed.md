# The Cluster Challenge - Detailed Mechanics

**Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: 2026-02-03  
**Status**: Development Draft

---

## 🎯 Core Concept

Instead of passively waiting for clusters to form organically, we **gamify cluster formation** by creating a competitive challenge that rewards the first communities to achieve true circular economy status.

---

## 🏆 The Challenge

**Announcement:** "The first 5 cities/communities to build a thriving local Stables economy win exclusive rewards and recognition."

**Challenge Period:** Starts in Phase 1 (Community Foundation), runs until 5 clusters qualify

---

## ✅ Cluster Qualification Criteria

To qualify as an official cluster, a community must achieve:

### **Tier 1: Emerging Cluster** (Bronze Status)
- **50+ active users** (at least 1 transaction in past 30 days)
- **10+ merchants** accepting Stables
- **100+ local transactions/week** (within the cluster)
- **Community champion** identified (local leader)

**Rewards:**
- Bronze Cluster Badge (NFT for all members)
- Featured on social media
- Dedicated Telegram/Discord channel
- Priority support from team

---

### **Tier 2: Thriving Cluster** (Silver Status)
- **100+ active users**
- **20+ merchants** across 3+ categories (food, retail, services)
- **500+ local transactions/week**
- **Local currency created** (e.g., sBerlin, sLagos)
- **Money velocity >2x/month** (average Stables changes hands 2+ times)

**Rewards:**
- Silver Cluster Badge (NFT)
- Custom local currency deployed
- Featured in all marketing materials
- Cluster champion gets Council seat nomination
- Early access to hardware device (top 10 users)

---

### **Tier 3: Self-Sustaining Cluster** (Gold Status)
- **200+ active users**
- **50+ merchants**
- **1,000+ local transactions/week**
- **Local currency circulating** (>50% of transactions use local currency)
- **Community governance active** (local decisions on currency parameters)

**Rewards:**
- Gold Cluster Badge (NFT)
- Guaranteed Council seat for cluster champion
- First 100 users get hardware device priority
- Cluster featured in case studies
- Cluster playbook co-authored with team
- Exclusive "Founding Cluster" status

### **Cluster Challenges (Mini-Quests)**

Each cluster can complete challenges to accelerate progress:

**Challenge 1: First Merchant**
- First cluster to onboard a merchant → 10 bonus points
- Reward: Merchant toolkit, marketing materials

**Challenge 2: First 100 Transactions**
- First cluster to hit 100 local transactions → 20 bonus points
- Reward: Featured social media post

**Challenge 3: Cross-Category Merchants**
- First cluster with merchants in 5+ categories → 30 bonus points
- Reward: Custom merchant directory page

**Challenge 4: Local Currency Launch**
- First cluster to launch local currency → 50 bonus points
- Reward: Dedicated marketing campaign

**Challenge 5: Community Event**
- Host local Stables meetup/event → 25 bonus points
- Reward: Event sponsorship, swag

## 👥 Cluster Champion Role

Each cluster needs a **Cluster Champion** - the local leader who:

**Responsibilities:**
- Organizes local community
- Onboards merchants
- Hosts events/meetups
- Provides feedback to team
- Represents cluster in Council (if qualified)

**Benefits:**
- Direct line to core team
- Early access to features
- Council seat nomination (Silver+)
- Guaranteed Council seat (Gold)
- Hardware device priority
- Recognition in all materials

**Selection:**
- Self-nominated or community-elected
- Must be active user (50+ transactions)
- Must onboard 3+ merchants
- Must host 1+ community event

## 🌍 Geographic Distribution Strategy

**We don't pick winners - communities compete organically**

**How it works:**
1. **Open announcement** - Any community can participate
2. **Self-organization** - Communities form around local champions
3. **Transparent metrics** - Real-time leaderboard shows progress
4. **First-come, first-served** - First 5 to qualify win

**Expected distribution:**
- Mix of developed and developing economies
- Different currencies (EUR, USD, local)
- Different cultures and use cases
- Proof that model works globally

## 💰 Financing Early Adopter Bonuses

### **Option 1: NFT Treasury (Recommended)**

**Mechanism:**
- Launch limited NFT collection (e.g., 1,000 NFTs)
- Sales fund cluster challenge rewards
- Transparent treasury management
- Community oversight

**NFT Tiers:**
- **Supporter NFT** ($50) - Funds general rewards
- **Champion NFT** ($200) - Funds cluster champion bonuses
- **Founder NFT** ($500) - Funds hardware device production

**Use of Funds:**
- 40% - Cluster challenge rewards (badges, bonuses, devices)
- 30% - Hardware device manufacturing
- 20% - External security audit
- 10% - Community events and marketing

**Transparency:**
- Public wallet address
- Quarterly spending reports
- Community vote on major expenses

---

### **Option 2: Donation-Based Funding**

**Mechanism:**
- Accept voluntary donations from believers
- Allocate donations to reward pool
- Transparent spending

**Donation Tiers:**
- $10 - Supporter (name on website)
- $50 - Contributor (special badge)
- $200 - Champion (early device access)
- $500+ - Founder (Council nomination)

**Challenges:**
- Unpredictable funding
- May not reach targets
- Requires ongoing fundraising

---

### **Option 3: Protocol Fee Reserve (Long-term)**

**Mechanism:**
- Small portion of transaction fees (e.g., 0.1%) goes to reward pool
- Funds accumulate over time
- Used for ongoing cluster support

**Advantages:**
- Self-sustaining
- Aligns incentives (more usage = more rewards)
- No external funding needed

**Challenges:**
- Only works after launch
- Can't fund early clusters
- Requires governance approval

---

### **Recommended Hybrid Approach:**

**Phase 1 (Pre-Launch):**
- NFT treasury funds initial cluster challenge
- Covers first 5 clusters + hardware devices
- One-time fundraising

**Phase 2 (Post-Launch):**
- Protocol fee reserve funds ongoing cluster support
- Sustainable long-term model
- Community-governed allocation

## 💡 Why This Works

**1. Creates Urgency**
- "First 5 clusters" creates FOMO
- Competition drives action
- Clear deadline (implicit)

**2. Leverages Community Energy**
- Champions emerge naturally
- Communities self-organize
- Peer pressure to participate

**3. Generates Organic Marketing**
- Cities competing = media interest
- User-generated content
- Word of mouth
- Social proof

**4. Proves the Model**
- Real-world validation
- Multiple use cases
- Different geographies
- Documented playbook

**5. Identifies Natural Leaders**
- Cluster champions = future Council members
- Community-vetted leadership
- Proven track record
