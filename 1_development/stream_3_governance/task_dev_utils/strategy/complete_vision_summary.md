### **Current Financial System:**
- ❌ Banks control access
- ❌ Opaque decision-making

- ❌ Expensive (high fees, intermediaries)
- ❌ Discriminatory (bias in lending)

### **On-Chain Financial System:**
- ✅ Algorithms control access (transparent rules)
- ✅ Transparent (see all flows, fees, interest)
- ✅ Instant (seconds for credit decisions)
- ✅ Cheap (no intermediaries)
- ✅ Fair (code doesn't discriminate)
- ✅ Includes everyone (just need internet + wallet)
