### 5. [governance_token_weighting.md](file:///C:/Users/Charles/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/Stables/1_development/strategy/governance_token_weighting.md)
**Time-Weighted Voting System**

**Purpose**: Empower small long-term holders through time-weighted voting power.

**Formula:**
```
Voting Power = Token Amount × Token Multiplier × Time Multiplier
```

**Token Multipliers:**
- Stables (stablecoins): 1x
- r-Tokens (staked in coverage pool): 2x
- xWiniwa (leveraged holders): 3x

**Time Multiplier:**
```
Time Multiplier = 1 + (Hold Duration in Days / 365)
Maximum: 5x at 4 years
```

**Example:**
- Small holder: 1,000 tokens held 2 years = 3,000 votes
- Whale: 4,000 tokens held 1 day = 4,000 votes
- Result: Small holder has 75% of whale's power with 25% of tokens

**Anti-Gaming:**
- Transfer resets hold duration
- Snapshot-based voting
- Minimum 30-day hold to submit proposals

**Council Structure:**
- 20 seats: 5 founding, 10 elected, 5 merit-based
- Voting types: Simple majority (>50%), Supermajority (>66%), Unanimous + community

**Read this** to understand how governance will work and how voting power is calculated.

## 💼 Operations Documents

### 6. [merchant_fee_structure.md](file:///C:/Users/Charles/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/Stables/1_development/strategy/merchant_fee_structure.md)
**Merchant Listing Fees & Delisting Policy**

**Purpose**: Define merchant fee structure with grandfathering for early adopters.

**Fee Structure:**
- **Year 1**: Lifetime fee ($100) - all merchants grandfathered forever
- **Year 2+**: Annual fee ($50/year) - Council can adjust $25-$200

**Grandfathering Policy:**
- Early adopters (Year 1) never pay recurring fees
- Special "Founding Merchant" badge
- Protected from future fee changes
- Featured in marketing materials

**Delisting Grounds:**
- Inactivity (12 months no transactions)
- Business closed/disappeared
- Fraud or misconduct
- Non-payment of annual fee (Year 2+ merchants only)

**Delisting Process:**
- Warnings at 10 months, 11 months
- Automatic delisting at 12 months
- Appeal process available
- Can reactivate anytime (pays current fee)

**Council Authority:**
- CAN adjust fees for NEW merchants ($25-$200 range)
- CAN adjust delisting procedures
- CANNOT change grandfathered terms (immutable)

**Read this** to understand merchant economics and lifecycle management.
