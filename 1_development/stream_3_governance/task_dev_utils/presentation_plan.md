# Stables Presentation Plan (v1)

## Goal
Create a compelling, single-page "scrollytelling" presentation (or GitHub README structure) to introduce **Stables** to the general public.

## Technical Constraints
*   **Format**: HTML5 (Single File) or Markdown.
*   **Styling**: Glassmorphism (Dark Slate `#0b0f14` + Neon Cyan `#67e8f9`).
*   **Assets**: STRICTLY use `brand_masters` (No new creations).
*   **Tone**: Sovereign, Professional, Simple.

## The 5-Slide Structure

### Slide 1: Identity (The Hook)
*   **Visual**: Large `master_symbol.png` (Center or Left).
*   **Headline**: **STABLES** (using `master_wordmark_transparent.png` or text implementation).
*   **Sub-headline**: "The Money Platform".
*   **Action**: "Scroll to Enter" or simple arrow.

### Slide 2: The Core Promise
*   **Tagline Reference**: "Money that is truly yours."
*   **Narrative**:
    > "In a world of surveillance and frozen accounts, Stables restores your power. It is digital cash that behaves like physical cash."
*   **Visual**: Subtle background glow or abstract connection nodes.

### Slide 3: The 3 Pillars (The "Why")
*   **Structure**: 3 Columns/Cards (Glassmorphism).
*   **Card 1**: **SECURE**
    *   "mathematically proven safety on the L1."
*   **Card 2**: **PSEUDONYMOUS**
    *   "No names. No banks. Just addresses."
*   **Card 3**: **UNSTOPPABLE**
    *   "No remote shut-off switch. You own the node."

### Slide 4: The Technology (Trust)
*   **Headline**: "Built on Minima"
*   **Copy**:
    > "Stables is a **MiniDapp**. It runs directly on your device, powered by the Minima compact blockchain. No central servers. No middlemen."
*   **Differentiation**: Focus on "Local execution" vs "Cloud dependency".

### Slide 5: The Call to Action (Join)
*   **Headline**: "Join the Council."
*   **Buttons**:
    *   [Primary] **X (Twitter)**: `@StablesCouncil`
    *   [Secondary] **GitHub**: `github.com/Charles0xhorizonxyz/stablescouncil`
*   **Footer**: "Stables v1.0 | A Sovereign Initiative"
