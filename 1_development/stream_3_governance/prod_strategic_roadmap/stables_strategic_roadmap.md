## 🎯 VISION & MISSION

### **The Vision**
> A global banking solution accessible to everyone, everywhere. Money that is truly yours - secure, pseudonymous, and unstoppable.

### **The Mission**
> Build a community-driven, self-custody banking platform on Minima that serves the general public with a focus on simplicity, security, and financial freedom.

### **The Device (Long-Term Goal)**
> A dedicated banking device with a Minima node on a chip - your personal bank that fits in your pocket, works offline, and answers to no one but you.

#### **App Development**

**MVP Features:**
- Wallet creation and management
- Winiwa → Stablecoin minting (sUSD, sEUR, sCAD, sGBP, sJPY, sCNY)
- Winiwa → xWiniwa (leveraged position, no liquidation)
- Send/receive payments
- Multi-currency support
- QR code payments
- Transaction history

**r-Token System:**
- Deposit stablecoins into rebalance pools
- Earn yield from transaction fees
- **30-day yield display** (prominent at top of app)
- **One-click "Invest Now" button**
- Automatic conversion to xWiniwa during rebalancing

**UI/UX Priorities:**
- **Non-crypto friendly** (no jargon)
- **Simple onboarding** (5 minutes max)
- **Clear value display** (portfolio in real Minima value)
- **Node name identity** (automatic from Minima node)
- **Hidden achievements** (discoverable keys)

**Technical Integration:**
- Real Minima price API
- Transaction fee collection for r-Tokens
- Coverage ratio monitoring
- Rebalancing mechanism

**Testing:**
- Internal testing (team)
- Closed alpha (50-100 users)
- Bug fixes and iterations
- Security audit (basic)

**Deliverable:** Working MVP ready for Quest Phase 1
