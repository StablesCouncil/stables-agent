## 1. The Constitutional Posture

### 1.1 Sovereign Context

- Minima is treated as the sovereign base layer of a borderless digital nation
- Minima token is the native currency of that nation

### 1.2 Stables Structural Identity

Stables is positioned as:

- The monetary coordination layer built within Minima's sovereign territory
- A sovereign banking infrastructure protocol built on Minima standards

Stables is **not** framed as a replacement of Minima. The constitution avoids any posture that would imply token-layer supremacy.

### 1.3 Transition Doctrine

Stables is explicitly a **coordination bridge**:

- It aligns current-world liabilities (USD, CAD, etc.) with Minima-native collateral
- It supports a long-term horizon where Minima becomes day-to-day currency for general public usage

We do not call Stables "temporary" in constitutional language. Instead we encode a **transition doctrine** that preserves strategic flexibility.

## 3. Foundational Decisions Taken

This section is the definitive checklist of decisions taken so far.

### 3.1 Collateral Decision *(constitutional, permanent)*

Minima is the only base collateral of the monetary core. No other asset inclusion exists at the monetary core. **Period.**

This is a sovereignty decision. It is not open-ended. It is not "future extensible."

### 3.2 Solvency Invariant *(constitutional, permanent)*

> Stable Supply ≤ Minima Collateral Value (oracle-valued)

- Minting requires invariant validation before execution
- No issuance path bypasses this constraint

### 3.3 Redemption Philosophy *(constitutional, permanent)*

Redemption is always truthful to the balance sheet:

> Backing Ratio = Assets / Liabilities

| Condition | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| Backing ≥ 1 | Redemption at par |
| Backing < 1 | Redemption at backing ratio |

This defines a **floating redemption mechanism**.

### 3.4 Peg Philosophy *(constitutional posture)*

The peg is an **equilibrium outcome**, not a defended promise. Market participants arbitrage pool price vs. redemption value when backing supports it. The protocol enforces balance-sheet truth.

### 3.5 Mechanical Continuity *(constitutional, permanent)*

The protocol is a mechanically continuous system. It does not rely on discretionary crisis modes.

Market stress is resolved through:
- Deterministic balance-sheet adjustment
- Floating redemption
- Equity absorption and recapitalisation incentives

**Not** governance action.

### 3.6 No Discretionary Emergency Logic *(constitutional, permanent)*

- No crisis regime switch
- No discretionary protocol pause for market stress
- No "depeg switches"

### 3.7 Oracle Integrity Gating *(allowed, bounded)*

We distinguish:

| Condition | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| **Market stress** | Floating redemption (continuous) |
| **Oracle integrity failure** | Minting may be gated for measurement integrity |

Key constraint: **gating affects issuance only; redemption remains continuous.** This is integrity protection, not peg defence.

### 3.8 No Forced Conversion *(constitutional decision)*

No forced stable-to-xMinima conversion as a crisis trigger. The system remains continuous rather than regime-switching.

### 3.9 Equity Layer (xMinima) — Structural Role

- xMinima represents the residual equity claim: **Assets − Liabilities**
- It absorbs volatility and recapitalises the system through market dynamics
- Dilution functions as recapitalisation
- This is a **structural absorber**, not a discretionary rescue tool

### 3.10 Treasury Role — Bounded Economic Participant

- Treasury may act as a rational economic participant when opportunities exist
- Treasury does **not** change monetary rules
- Treasury does **not** act as peg defender

### 3.11 Coverage Participation — Voluntary Resilience Layer

- Coverage participation is voluntary
- It enhances resilience through alignment and yield participation
- It does **not** replace the solvency invariant
- It does **not** trigger emergency states

### 3.12 Governance Scope — Hard Limitation

Critical monetary rules are encoded and excluded from governance control:
- Solvency invariant
- Redemption formula
- Issuance constraint
- Oracle aggregation logic

Governance operates within coded boundaries and focuses on expansion and ecosystem coordination.

### 3.13 Council Definition *(role posture)*

The Council becomes:
- **Architect** of expansion
- **Coordinator** of ecosystem
- **Guardian** of invariant integrity

The Council is **not** a monetary operator.

### 3.15 Physical Arbitrage as Structural Peg Force *(settled, 2026-03-06)*

Merchant acceptance creates a structural peg floor: when stablecoins trade below par, participants buy discounted stables and use them at face value with merchants (effective discount on goods). The larger the depeg, the stronger this corrective force.

This mechanism does **not** rely on merchant ignorance. Merchants are assumed fully informed.

### 3.16 No Information Asymmetry *(ethical constraint, 2026-03-06)*

We reject any peg mechanism that depends on exploiting merchant ignorance. The system must work when all participants are fully informed. If a merchant adjusts prices to reflect a depeg, that is the transparent equivalent of inflation — visible, honest, and temporary.

### 3.17 Opt-In, No Coercion *(constitutional posture, 2026-03-06)*

Participation is voluntary. Exit is unconditional. No one is punished for choosing not to participate. The system survives through intrinsic attractiveness, not external enforcement. This is a foundational contrast with fiat systems where coercion (legal tender laws, taxation) enforces participation.

### 3.18 MINIMA as Native Stable *(transition doctrine, 2026-03-06)*

MINIMA is listed alongside USDs, EURs, CADs, IRTs from inception — stable relative to itself (1 MINIMA = 1 MINIMA). The fiat-pegged instruments are transitional bridges. When the merchant economy prices directly in MINIMA, Stables has fulfilled its purpose.

### 3.19 Access to Money as Human Right *(foundational conviction, 2026-03-06)*

Money should not require permission, cost anything to hold, depend on infrastructure that can be cut off, or discriminate. This is the WHY behind the protocol.

### 3.20 Council and Coverage Fund as Market Participants Only *(structural, 2026-03-06)*

The Council and Coverage Fund act only as market participants — arbitrageurs, speculators, investors. The protocol's structural soundness does not depend on them. They enhance but are not required.

### 3.21 Transition Doctrine *(constitutional, 2026-03-06, revised)*

The Charter situates Stables within a full arc of monetary evolution:

- **Stage −2 — Commodity Money**: Community-based, no coercion
- **Stage −1 — Sound Money Under Central Authority**: State-backed, coercion enters
- **Stage 0 — Fiat Under Centralised Control**: Where we are today. Mandatory, unaccountable
- **Stage 1 — Stables**: Sovereign opt-in merchant payment system. Bridge from fiat. *Participants* choose to join.
- **Stage 2 — Minima-Native Economy**: Merchants price in Minima. Sovereignty achieved but limited by concentration. **Not required to reach Stage 3** — it is the most natural path but not the only one.
- **Stage 3 — Circular Horizon**: Language shifts from *participants* to **humans**. Every human receives monetary power by right. System is circular, egalitarian, planet-preserving. **We do not know how to build this yet.** We name it honestly.
- **Beyond Stage 3**: Further evolution is expected. No stage is final.

The path is non-linear. What matters is the direction: coercion → sovereignty → universality → circularity.

### 3.22 Minima Concentration Acknowledged *(structural honesty, 2026-03-06)*

Minima's supply is concentrated by historical circumstance. The Charter explicitly acknowledges this limitation as the reason Minima-native economy (Stage 2) is not the final destination.


### 3.14 Two Destinations *(publicly disclosable anchor)*

Only the destinations are treated as publicly disclosed anchors:

**Technical Destination — Infrastructure Sovereignty:**
Minima-native collateral, Integritas anchoring, on-chain transparency, node participation, code-first monetary logic, dedicated sovereign banking device embedding a Minima node on chip.

**Financial Destination — Economic Sovereignty:**
Synthetic stable representations, merchant settlement network, liability matching instruments, on-chain credit primitives, lending infrastructure, treasury-driven equity reinforcement, comprehensive banking primitives over time.

**Technical sovereignty enables financial sovereignty.**

## 9. Summary (Quick Onboarding)

> Stables is the monetary coordination layer of the Minima nation: a sovereign banking infrastructure protocol built on Minima with immutable monetary invariants enforced by deterministic code. The monetary core is permanently Minima-only, with a hard solvency invariant and floating redemption that always reflects the backing ratio. The protocol is mechanically continuous, contains no discretionary emergency logic, and resolves market stress through deterministic balance-sheet adjustment and distributed recapitalisation via equity participation. Governance does not operate the monetary core; the Council stewards expansion, coordinates ecosystem growth, and guards invariant integrity. Stables acts as a bridge between current-world liabilities and Minima-native economic coordination, with a long-term horizon in which Minima becomes a day-to-day currency used by the general public.
