## How to Navigate

**Starting fresh?** Read the Charter, then the Companion doc.

**Looking for economic mechanics?** Charter Art. II → then `working_papers/economic_model_summary.md`

**Looking for peg soundness reasoning?** Charter Art. IV → then `working_papers/financial_soundness.md`

**Looking for governance design?** Charter Art. VII → then `working_papers/governance_token_weighting.md`

**Drafting or reviewing?** Read the Companion doc for tone rules and settled decisions.
