PREAMBLE
Sovereign Context, Structural Identity, and Transition Doctrine

Stables is a sovereign banking infrastructure protocol built on Minima.

Minima is the sovereign base layer of a borderless digital nation.
Minima token is the native currency of that nation.

Stables is the monetary coordination layer built within that sovereign territory.

Each participant account structurally represents its own bank through:

• self-custody
• deterministic execution
• transparent solvency
• direct on-chain settlement

Stables operates as a coordination layer facilitating alignment between existing liability structures and Minima-native economic participation.

Its horizon is a Minima-native economy in which Minima functions as a day-to-day currency used by the general public.

This Charter is issued as a foundational draft for community feedback and active contribution, prior to formal adoption by a diversified Council.

ARTICLE I
Mission and Dual Destination
I.1 Core Objective

Enable individuals and merchants to be their own bank through:

• sovereign infrastructure
• mathematical solvency
• deterministic monetary mechanics
• merchant-driven economic relevance
• progressive decentralisation

I.2 Structural Identity

Stables is:

A sovereign banking infrastructure protocol
on Minima

governed by deterministic code
with a Council that stewards expansion
and manages systemic risk within invariant boundaries
while acting as a bridge between current-world liabilities and Minima-native economic coordination.

Stables is a mechanically continuous system.

I.3 Dual Destination Framework
I.3.1 Technical Destination — Infrastructure Sovereignty

Full technical sovereignty through:

• Minima-native collateral
• Integritas anchoring
• on-chain transparency
• node participation

• dedicated sovereign banking device embedding a Minima node on chip

I.3.2 Financial Destination — Economic Sovereignty

Full financial sovereignty through:

• synthetic stable representations
• merchant settlement network
• liability matching instruments
• on-chain credit primitives
• lending infrastructure
• treasury-driven equity reinforcement
• comprehensive on-chain banking functions, evolving over time

Technical sovereignty enables financial sovereignty.

ARTICLE II
Monetary Constitution
Immutable Core
II.1 Minima-Only Base Collateral

The monetary core uses Minima as exclusive base collateral.

This is constitutionally permanent.

No other asset inclusion exists at the monetary core. Period.

II.2 Solvency Invariant

Formal condition:

Stable Supply ≤ Minima Collateral Value (oracle-valued)

Minting requires invariant validation before execution.

No issuance path bypasses this constraint.

II.3 Floating Redemption

Backing Ratio = Assets / Liabilities

Redemption always equals the backing ratio.

• backing ≥ 1 → redemption at par
• backing < 1 → redemption at backing ratio

II.4 Deterministic Crisis Continuity

Market stress resolves through deterministic balance-sheet adjustment.

System behavior remains continuous:

• backing ratio adjusts
• redemption floats
• equity absorbs volatility
• recapitalisation emerges through incentives

II.5 Oracle Aggregation and Issuance Integrity

Oracle aggregation logic is embedded at protocol level.

Issuance references stabilized oracle values.

If oracle integrity score falls below threshold:

• minting may be gated until measurement stabilizes
• redemption remains continuous

This gating protects measurement integrity, not price.

II.6 Mechanical Identity of the Core

Stables is:

A floating collateralised synthetic monetary layer
with deterministic solvency enforcement
and distributed recapitalisation.

Stables is:

A floating collateralized synthetic monetary layer
anchored in Minima
driven by merchant adoption
stabilized through equity participation
governed by coded constraints
and fully transparent in operation.

ARTICLE III
Structural Layers and Modularity

Minima is the monetary base.
Stables is the structured banking layer.
Applications can build above.
Expansion remains modular.

Layer model:

• Layer 0 — Minima sovereign base layer
• Layer 1 — Stables immutable monetary core
• Layer 2 — Stables evolvable banking coordination layer
• Layer 3 — Applications and integrations

This layering preserves sovereignty and prevents doctrinal rigidity.

Growth Architecture

Stables is driven by merchant adoption.

The flywheel begins with Agents.

Agents → Merchants → Treasury growth → Equity depth → Stable minting → Transaction activity → Fees → Coverage participation → Reinforcement → Expanded merchant network.

Economic relevance grows endogenously.

Merchant adoption anchors equilibrium depth.

3. The most important decisions already locked

This section is the definitive checklist of decisions taken so far.

3.1 Collateral decision (constitutional, permanent)

Minima is the only base collateral of the monetary core.

No other asset inclusion exists at the monetary core. Period.

This is a sovereignty decision.
It is not open-ended. It is not “future extensible.”

3.2 Solvency invariant (constitutional, permanent)

The solvency invariant is permanent and enforced by code:

Stable Supply ≤ Minima Collateral Value (oracle-valued)

Minting requires invariant validation before execution

No issuance path bypasses this constraint

3.3 Redemption philosophy (constitutional, permanent)

Redemption is always truthful to the balance sheet:

Backing Ratio = Assets / Liabilities

Redemption equals this ratio at all times:

backing ≥ 1 → redemption at par

backing < 1 → redemption at backing ratio

This defines a floating redemption mechanism.

3.4 Peg philosophy (constitutional posture)

The peg is an equilibrium outcome, not a defended promise.

Market participants arbitrage pool price vs redemption value when backing supports it.

The protocol enforces balance-sheet truth.

3.5 Mechanical continuity (constitutional, permanent)

The protocol is a mechanically continuous system.

It does not rely on discretionary crisis modes.

Market stress is resolved through:

deterministic balance-sheet adjustment

floating redemption

equity absorption and recapitalisation incentives

Not governance action.

3.6 No discretionary emergency logic (constitutional, permanent)

The monetary core contains no discretionary emergency logic.

There is no crisis regime switch.

There is no discretionary protocol pause for market stress.

There are no “depeg switches.”

3.7 Oracle integrity gating (allowed, bounded)

We distinguish:

A) market stress
B) oracle integrity failure

Market stress → floating redemption (continuous)
Oracle integrity failure → minting may be gated for measurement integrity

Key constraint:

gating affects issuance only

redemption remains continuous

This is integrity protection, not peg defense.

3.8 No forced conversion mechanics (constitutional decision)

There is no forced stable-to-xMinima conversion as a crisis trigger.

The system remains continuous rather than regime-switching.

3.9 Equity layer (xMinima) — structural role

xMinima represents the residual equity claim: Assets − Liabilities

It absorbs volatility and recapitalises the system through market dynamics

Dilution functions as recapitalisation

This is not framed as a discretionary rescue tool.
It is a structural absorber.

3.10 Treasury role — bounded economic participant

Treasury may act as a rational economic participant when opportunities exist

Treasury does not change monetary rules

Treasury does not act as peg defender

3.11 Coverage participation — voluntary resilience layer

Coverage participation is voluntary

It enhances resilience through alignment and yield participation

It does not replace the solvency invariant

It does not trigger emergency states

3.12 Governance scope — hard limitation

Critical monetary rules are encoded and excluded from governance control:

solvency invariant

redemption formula

issuance constraint

oracle aggregation logic

Governance operates within coded boundaries and focuses on expansion and ecosystem coordination.

3.13 Council definition (role posture)

The Council becomes:

Architect of expansion

Coordinator of ecosystem

Guardian of invariant integrity

The Council is not a monetary operator.
