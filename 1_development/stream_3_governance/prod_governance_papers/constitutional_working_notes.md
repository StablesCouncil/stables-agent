## Sovereign Architecture Framing

You are building: the monetary coordination layer of the Minima nation.

And possibly: a transitional bridge between today's liability-denominated world and a Minima-native economy.

That is a very different constitutional posture.

- Minima is the sovereign base layer
- Minima token is the native currency
- Stables is the monetary system built within that sovereign territory
- You are not the central bank of Minima
- You are the architect of a banking system operating on Minima standards

That means:
- Minima is the monetary base
- Stables is the structured banking layer
- Applications can build above
- Expansion is modular

This must be articulated carefully because it prevents:
- Perception of monetary imperialism
- Token-layer supremacy claims

## Transition Doctrine (Raw Thinking)

Stables exists to:
- Align current-world liabilities (USD, CAD, etc.)
- With Minima-native collateral
- Until economic coordination evolves

The ultimate horizon is Minima-native denomination.

But the Constitution must handle this delicately.

We cannot declare: "We are temporary" — because constitutional documents anchor permanence.

Instead, we phrase it as:

> Stables operates as a coordination layer facilitating alignment between existing liability structures and Minima-native economic participation.

This preserves strategic flexibility.

We are not claiming: "We are the final system."

We are saying: "We build the sovereign banking infrastructure compatible with Minima."

This humility strengthens the Constitution because it prevents doctrinal rigidity.

## Historical Framing of Money

*(Note: These ideas have been incorporated into the Charter Preamble.)*

Back to the future — we need to situate our solution historically. Money has never truly "existed" as an independent thing. Humans have always exchanged goods, and at some point began using intermediary assets that had value on their own. We need to identify which banking era corresponds to our structure, setting aside the technology aspect.

Our solution is transitional. It is only a bridge between two systems — the current one and the next. The next one could be the usage of Minima directly, but this stage will probably be followed by yet another, because Minima holding is not sufficiently distributed. A better system would be one that mostly has the characteristics of Minima but with a distribution that ensures every participant holds a fair proportion of the common wealth.
