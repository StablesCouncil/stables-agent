ℹ️ How voting power is calculated
Voting Power = Amount × Token Multiplier × Time Multiplier

Token multipliers: Wables 1× · cover pool 2× · xWiniwa 3×
Time multiplier: 1 + (days held ÷ 365), max 5× at 4 years
Transferring tokens resets the hold duration to zero.

ℹ️ Who sets these parameters?
Protocol parameters are set by the Council through on-chain governance votes. Any xWiniwa, Wables or cover pool holder can vote. Changes require a majority weighted by Voting Power (amount × token multiplier × time multiplier). Emergency parameters may be fast-tracked with 80% supermajority.
