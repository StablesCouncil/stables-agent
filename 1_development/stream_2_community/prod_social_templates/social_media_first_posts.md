## Notes & Best Practices

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Messaging Rules Compliance Check**
> - ✅ Brand name: "Stables" (never "Stables")
> - ✅ Governance: "Council" (never "DAO")
> - ✅ Tagline: "Money that is truly yours. Secure, Pseudonymous and Unstoppable."
> - ✅ Target: General public (accessible language, no excessive jargon)
> - ✅ Hashtag exception: Technical terms allowed as hashtags only

> [!TIP]
> **Engagement Optimization**
> - Post X content during peak hours (9 AM - 12 PM UTC, 5 PM - 8 PM UTC)
> - Instagram performs best 11 AM - 1 PM and 7 PM - 9 PM (user's timezone +3)
> - Always respond to comments within first 2 hours
> - Use Instagram Stories to drive traffic to main post
