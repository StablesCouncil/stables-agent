## Content Strategy Notes

**Alignment with Multi-Platform Strategy:**
- Music playlist fits **Phase 5: Community Proof** (cultural content)
- Shows human side of the project
- Builds emotional connection
- Can be shared across platforms

**Best Practices:**
- Curate carefully - music reflects brand values
- Consider copyright/licensing for any music you create
- Sharing others' music = playlist curation (generally okay)
- Add thoughtful descriptions to each video explaining why it resonates
- Engage with artists/creators when possible

**Themes to Consider:**
- Freedom & liberty
- Self-determination
- Building & creating
- Community & collaboration
- Resilience & persistence
- Privacy & autonomy
- Hope & optimism
