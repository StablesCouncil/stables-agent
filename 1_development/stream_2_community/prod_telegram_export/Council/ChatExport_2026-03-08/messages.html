We are pleased to share our banking system overview, a visual map of how Stables works. We hope it helps you visualize the structure we are putting in place, and we of course welcome any feedback.

Please note that this is a draft. It is far from definitive and is meant to be discussed and refined together with the community.

At the centre is the circular economy, where Stables are used as the payment mean. Around it, every entry point, whether financial by minting xMinima and any Stables from Minima, or by bringing goods and services to the system by accepting Stables as a Merchant. There are also every exit, and every protocol layer that makes it possible.

Also supporting the network are two protocol pillars. The Coverage Fund, funded by transaction fees generated within the circular economy, ensures the stability of the system. The Liquidity Fund provides market price discovery for xMinima, the equity layer of the protocol, allowing the system to reflect its own value in real time.

The Network of Everybody's Own Bank forms the Stables banking system.

Every token holder governs the protocol via the Minima blockchain by having a say as a Stables Council member.
