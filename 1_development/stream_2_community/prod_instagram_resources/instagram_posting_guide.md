## How to Post on Instagram (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Open Instagram App
- Instagram works best from a mobile phone
- If you don't have the app, download "Instagram" from your app store

### Step 2: Create New Post
1. Tap the **+** button at the bottom center
2. Select **Post**
3. Tap **Select Multiple** (icon with overlapping squares in top right)

### Step 3: Select Your Images
1. Navigate to where you saved the 4 slides
2. Tap them **in order**: Slide 1 → Slide 2 → Slide 3 → Slide 4
3. Tap **Next** (top right)

### Step 4: Skip Filters (Optional)
- You can skip the filter screen by tapping **Next** again
- The images are already designed and don't need filters

### Step 5: Add Your Caption
Paste this caption (from the social media first posts document):

```
Stables is here. 💫

Money that is truly yours. Secure. Pseudonymous. Unstoppable.

Imagine money that:
→ Moves as fast as a message
→ Can never be frozen
→ Charges no hidden fees
→ Requires no permission

This is Stables. A new kind of money platform built on Minima.

The future is pseudonymous. The future is unstoppable.

Money Platform.

🔗 Link in bio to explore the full vision
```

### Step 6: Add Link to Bio
Before posting, update your bio:
1. Go to your profile
2. Tap **Edit Profile**
3. In the **Website** field, paste your GitHub presentation URL
4. Tap **Done**

### Step 7: Post Hashtags
**Option A**: In the caption (simpler for beginners)
- Add these hashtags at the bottom of your caption:
```
#Stables #MoneyPlatform #Minima #FinancialFreedom #Cryptocurrency #DeFi #Web3 #FutureOfMoney #DigitalCurrency #Blockchain
```

**Option B**: In first comment (cleaner look)
- Post without hashtags
- Immediately comment on your own post with the hashtags above

### Step 8: Share!
1. Tap **Share** (bottom right)
2. Your carousel is now live! 🎉

## Instagram Tips for Beginners

### Carousel Best Practices
- ✅ People can swipe left to see all 4 images
- ✅ Carousels get 3x more engagement than single images
- ✅ Instagram shows "1/4" indicator so people know to swipe

### After Posting
- **Respond to comments** within the first 2 hours (Instagram rewards engagement)
- **Share to Stories**: Tap your post → paper airplane icon → "Add post to story"
- **Pin it**: Go to your profile → tap the post → three dots → "Pin to profile"

### What "Link in Bio" Means
- Instagram doesn't allow clickable links in captions
- "Link in bio" means people go to your profile and click the website link
- That's why we added the GitHub URL to your bio in Step 6
