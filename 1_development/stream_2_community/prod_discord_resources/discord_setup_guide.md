## 🎯 DISCORD STRATEGY

### **When to Launch Discord:**
According to the communication plan, Discord launches in **Phase 4** (Technical Details phase), after:
- Instagram, X, Facebook, Telegram are established
- Community has grown to 15,000+ followers
- Technical audience is ready for deep dives

### **Discord Purpose:**
- **Technical community hub** (developers, contributors)
- **Code discussions** (GitHub integration)
- **Deep technical explanations**
- **Developer support**
- **Open-source collaboration**

### **Discord is NOT for:**
- General community (use Telegram)
- Marketing/announcements (use X, Instagram, Facebook)
- Customer support (use Telegram Group)

## 👥 ROLES STRUCTURE

### **Role Hierarchy (Top to Bottom):**

#### **1. Founder** (Red #ff0000)
- Full admin permissions
- Assign to: Charles (you)

#### **2. Team** (Cyan #67e8f9)
- Admin permissions
- Assign to: Core team members

#### **3. Moderator** (Purple #a855f7)
- Moderate permissions (kick, ban, mute, manage messages)
- Assign to: Trusted community members

#### **4. Developer** (Green #10b981)
- No special permissions (just a badge)
- Assign to: Active contributors to GitHub

#### **5. Quester** (Gold #fbbf24)
- No special permissions (just a badge)
- Assign to: Active Quest participants

#### **6. Ambassador** (Blue #3b82f6)
- No special permissions (just a badge)
- Assign to: Community ambassadors

#### **7. Member** (Gray #6b7280)
- Basic permissions (read, post, react)
- Auto-assigned when users accept rules

#### **8. Muted** (Dark Gray #374151)
- Can read but not post
- Used for temporary mutes

## 📊 COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

### **Moderation Philosophy:**
- **Be helpful, not harsh** - Guide, don't punish
- **Warn first** - Give people a chance to correct
- **Escalate gradually** - Warning → Mute → Ban
- **Document everything** - Use mod logs
- **Be consistent** - Apply rules fairly

### **Response Times:**
- **Critical issues** (scams, harassment): Immediate
- **Rule violations**: Within 1 hour
- **Questions**: Within 24 hours
- **Feature requests**: Acknowledge within 48 hours

### **Community Health Metrics:**
- **Active members**: Track daily/weekly active users
- **Message volume**: Monitor channel activity
- **Sentiment**: Watch for negativity or toxicity
- **Growth rate**: Track new members per week
