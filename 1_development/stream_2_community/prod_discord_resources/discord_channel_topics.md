## 📝 HOW TO ADD CHANNEL TOPICS

1. Right-click the channel → **Edit Channel**
2. Go to **Overview** tab
3. Find **Channel Topic** field
4. Copy the topic from above
5. Paste into the field
6. Click **Save Changes**
