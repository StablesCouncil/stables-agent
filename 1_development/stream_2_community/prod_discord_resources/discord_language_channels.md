## 📝 SETUP INSTRUCTIONS

### **Step 1: Create Category**
1. Right-click in channel list → **Create Category**
2. Name: `🌍 LANGUAGES`
3. Position: After GENERAL category, before TECHNICAL

### **Step 2: Create Channels**
For each language channel:
1. Click **+** next to LANGUAGES category
2. Choose **Text Channel**
3. Name the channel (e.g., `français`)
4. Click **Create Channel**

### **Step 3: Add Topics**
For each channel:
1. Right-click → **Edit Channel**
2. Go to **Overview** tab
3. Copy the **Channel Topic** from above
4. Paste into the field
5. Click **Save Changes**

### **Step 4: Post Pinned Messages**
For each channel:
1. Copy the **Pinned Message** from above
2. Paste into the channel
3. Right-click the message → **Pin Message**

### **Step 5: Set Slowmode** (Optional)
- Recommended: 10 seconds for all language channels
- Prevents spam while allowing conversation

## 🎯 WHY LANGUAGE CHANNELS?

**Benefits:**
- ✅ **Inclusive** - Everyone can discuss in their native language
- ✅ **Community Building** - Connect with local communities
- ✅ **Local Clusters** - Coordinate geographic clusters by language
- ✅ **Accessibility** - Lower barrier to entry for non-English speakers
- ✅ **Growth** - Tap into international markets

**Target Languages:**
- **French** - France, Belgium, Switzerland, Canada, Africa
- **German** - Germany, Austria, Switzerland
- **Turkish** - Turkey, large diaspora in Europe
- **Chinese** - China, Taiwan, Singapore, large global diaspora
- **International** - All other languages (Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, etc.)
