# Discord Channel Permissions Setup Guide
**Date:** 2026-01-21  
**Purpose:** Make information channels read-only for regular members

---

## 🔒 **Channels to Make Read-Only:**

- ✅ #welcome
- ✅ #rules
- ✅ #announcements
- ✅ #resources

---

## 📋 **Step-by-Step Instructions:**

### **For Each Channel Above:**

1. **Click the ⚙️ gear icon** next to the channel name
2. **Click "Permissions"** in the left sidebar
3. **Click on "@everyone"** in the Roles/Members list
4. **Scroll down** to find "Text Channel Permissions" section
5. **Find "Send Messages"** permission
6. **Click the ❌ (red X)** to deny
7. **Click "Save Changes"** at the bottom
8. **Repeat for next channel**

---

## ✅ **What This Does:**

- ❌ Regular members **cannot send messages**
- ✅ Regular members **can read messages**
- ✅ Regular members **can add reactions**
- ✅ Admins **can still post** (override permissions)

---

## 🎯 **Testing:**

To verify it's working:
1. Ask a friend to join your Discord
2. They try to type in #welcome
3. They should see: "You do not have permission to send messages in this channel"
