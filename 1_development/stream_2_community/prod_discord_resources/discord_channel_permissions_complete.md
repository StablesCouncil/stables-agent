## 📋 **COMPLETE PERMISSION LIST (Exact Discord Order)**

### **STEP 1: INFORMATION Category - Read-Only Channels**

**Channels:** #welcome, #rules, #announcements, #resources

1. Click ⚙️ next to **#welcome** → **Permissions** → **@everyone**
2. Set these permissions in order:

#### **General Category Permissions:**
- ✅ **View Channels** (green ✓)
- ❌ **Manage Channels** (red X)
- ❌ **Manage Permissions** (red X)
- ❌ **Manage Webhooks** (red X)

#### **Membership Permissions:**
- ❌ **Create Invite** (red X)

#### **Text Channel Permissions:**
- ❌ **Send Messages and Create Posts** (red X)
- ❌ **Send Messages in Threads and Posts** (red X)
- ❌ **Create Public Threads** (red X)
- ❌ **Create Private Threads** (red X)
- ❌ **Embed Links** (red X)
- ❌ **Attach Files** (red X)
- ✅ **Add Reactions** (green ✓) ← Let people react!
- ❌ **Use External Emoji** (red X)
- ❌ **Use External Stickers** (red X)
- ❌ **Mention @everyone, @here, and All Roles** (red X)
- ❌ **Manage Messages** (red X)
- ❌ **Pin Messages** (red X)
- ❌ **Bypass Slowmode** (red X)
- ❌ **Manage Threads and Posts** (red X)
- ✅ **Read Message History** (green ✓) ← Important!
- ❌ **Send Text-to-Speech Messages** (red X)
- ❌ **Send Voice Messages** (red X)
- ❌ **Create Polls** (red X)

#### **Voice Channel Permissions:**
- ❌ **Connect** (red X)
- ❌ **Speak** (red X)
- ❌ **Video** (red X)
- ❌ **Use Soundboard** (red X)
- ❌ **Use External Sounds** (red X)
- ❌ **Use Voice Activity** (red X)
- ❌ **Priority Speaker** (red X)
- ❌ **Mute Members** (red X)
- ❌ **Deafen Members** (red X)
- ❌ **Move Members** (red X)
- ❌ **Set Voice Channel Status** (red X)

#### **Apps Permissions:**
- ❌ **Use Application Commands** (red X)
- ❌ **Use Activities** (red X)
- ❌ **Use External Apps** (red X)

#### **Stage Channel Permissions:**
- ❌ **Request to Speak** (red X)

#### **Events Permissions:**
- ❌ **Create Events** (red X)
- ❌ **Manage Events** (red X)

3. **Click "Save Changes"**
4. **Click "Sync Permissions"** when prompted

✅ **Done! All INFORMATION channels are read-only.**
