Stables Community,

The My bank positioning tool is now live in the development stream. We are excited to share this update as it brings a new level of transparency to your portfolio stress testing. You can now simulate exactly how your holdings respond to Winiwa price volatility at any time.

There are two primary features to explore:

The custom range editor allows you to define your own stress test domain. By clicking the pen icon on the performance graph, you can set price points from minus 100 to plus 300 percent to see how your assets would behave in extreme scenarios.

The xWiniwa simulation is now powered by a full residual math model. This means the graph correctly shows the leveraged convexity of the position as Minima price moves. We have also ensured that simulated trades are value neutral at the current price, so your entries are accurate before the simulation begins.

Please test the interactive slider and let us know your thoughts. This is another step in building the tools for all of us to be our bank.
