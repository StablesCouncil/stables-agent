## 🔗 Finding Your Group Link

Before updating your bio, you need to get the shareable invite link for your Stables Community group:

### Step 1: Get the Group Link

1. Open your **Stables Community** Telegram group
2. Tap the group name at the top
3. Tap **"..."** (three dots) or **"Share"**
4. You'll see the invite link format:
   - If public: `t.me/StablesCommunity` (or whatever username you set)
   - If private: `t.me/joinchat/[unique-code]`
5. **Copy** this link

---

## ✏️ Updating Your Profile Bio

### On Mobile:

1. Open Telegram
2. Tap **Settings** (hamburger menu, bottom right)
3. Tap your name/profile
4. Tap **"Edit"**
5. Tap **"Bio"**
6. Paste the new bio (replace `[Add group link here]` with your actual group link)
7. Tap **"Done"** or **"✓"**

### On Desktop:

1. Open Telegram Desktop
2. Click **Settings** (three horizontal lines, top left)
3. Click **"Edit Profile"**
4. Click in the **Bio** field
5. Paste the new bio (replace `[Add group link here]` with your actual group link)
6. Click **"Save"**

## 💡 Note on Bio Character Limits

Telegram bio limits:
- **Maximum**: 70 characters (when viewing in search/username lookup)
- **Profile view**: 140 characters visible
- **Full bio**: Can be longer but gets truncated

If you hit the limit, use Option 1 (shortest format) above.
