## 📈 PILLAR 1: ORGANIC CONTENT EXCELLENCE

### 1.1 Content Optimization by Platform

#### Instagram (@StablesCouncil)
**Current Strategy:** 2-3 posts/week (Phase -2)  
**Growth Tactics:**

**A. Carousel Mastery**
- Create 5-10 slide carousels (Instagram favors these in algorithm)
- First slide: Eye-catching hook
- Last slide: Strong CTA ("Save this" / "Share with someone who needs this")
- Example topics:
  - "5 Reasons Banks Don't Want You to Know About Self-Custody"
  - "How to Explain Stables to Your Parents (in 10 slides)"
  - "The 3 Pillars Explained: A Visual Journey"

**B. Reels Strategy**
- **Weekly Reels** (starting Week 2): 15-30 second explainer videos
- Use trending audio with Stables messaging overlay
- Topics:
  - "POV: You just sent money across the world in 3 seconds"
  - "When your bank says 'business hours' but Stables says 'always open'"
  - "The moment you realize you ARE the bank"
- Optimize for:
  - Hook in first 1 second
  - Text overlay for sound-off viewing
  - Strong visual identity (dark slate/cyan)

**C. Stories Strategy**
- **Daily Stories** (starting Week 3):
  - Behind-the-scenes development
  - Polls: "Which feature matters most to you?"
  - Q&A sessions: "Ask us anything about Stables"
  - Countdown to beta testing
  - Reshare community posts (when available)

**D. Hashtag Strategy**
- **Primary Hashtags** (use on every post):
  - #Stables
  - #BuildInPublic
  - #BuiltOnMINIMA
  - #BeYourOwnBank
  
- **Rotating Hashtags** (5-10 per post, relevant to content):
  - #SelfCustody #FinancialFreedom #CryptoWallet
  - #DeFi #Web3 #Blockchain #Fintech
  - #MoneyFreedom #DigitalMoney #P2P
  - #Pseudonymous #Unstoppable #Secure

**E. Engagement Tactics**
- Respond to EVERY comment within 2 hours
- Ask questions in captions to drive comments
- Create "tag a friend who needs this" moments
- Feature user comments in Stories

**Growth Target:** 50-100 followers/week → 1,200-2,400 in 6 months

#### X / Twitter (@StablesCouncil)
**Current Strategy:** 3-5 posts/week (Phase -2)  
**Growth Tactics:**

**A. Thread Mastery**
- **2-3 threads per week** (educational deep dives)
- Structure:
  - Tweet 1: Hook (controversial or surprising statement)
  - Tweets 2-8: Value delivery
  - Final tweet: CTA + "Retweet if this was valuable"
- Example hooks:
  - "Your bank account isn't yours. Here's proof: 🧵"
  - "I spent 6 months building a money app. Here's what I learned about the financial system: 🧵"
  - "Most people don't understand what 'self-custody' means. Let me fix that: 🧵"

**B. Engagement Farming (Ethical)**
- **Daily engagement tweets:**
  - "What's the biggest problem with traditional banking? (Wrong answers only)"
  - "If you could redesign money from scratch, what would you change?"
  - "Unpopular opinion: [controversial but defensible take about finance]"
- Reply to EVERY response thoughtfully

**C. Quote Tweet Strategy**
- Monitor MINIMA's tweets → Quote tweet with Stables perspective
- Find viral fintech/crypto tweets → Add Stables angle
- Example: "This is exactly why we're building Stables. [Link to presentation]"

**D. Spaces Strategy**
- **Monthly Twitter Spaces** (starting Month 2):
  - "Ask Us Anything: Building Stables"
  - "The Future of Money: A Conversation"
  - Co-host with MINIMA team or aligned projects

**E. Hashtag Strategy**
- Use 2-3 hashtags max (X penalizes over-hashtagging)
- Focus on: #BuildInPublic #FinTech #DeFi
- Use crypto hashtags ONLY when appropriate for discovery

**F. Timing Optimization**
- Post at peak engagement times:
  - 8-10 AM EST (Europe + US East Coast)
  - 12-2 PM EST (Lunch break)
  - 6-8 PM EST (Evening engagement)

**Growth Target:** 100-200 followers/week → 2,400-4,800 in 6 months
