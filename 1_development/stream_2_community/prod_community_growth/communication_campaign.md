## 🎯 CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVES

### **Primary Goals:**
1. **Educate** non-crypto audience about self-custody banking
2. **Build trust** through transparency and positive messaging
3. **Grow community** across platforms (X, Facebook, Instagram, Telegram)
4. **Create clusters** (geographic concentrations with circulating economies)
5. **Onboard merchants** (businesses accepting Stables)

### **Success Metrics:**
- Community growth (followers, engagement, clusters)
- Educational reach (content views, shares)
- Merchant interest (inquiries, signups)
- Geographic clustering (concentrated users)
