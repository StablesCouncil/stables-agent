# Stables X Thread Draft: Let it Rain (Retrocession)

Post 1
Access to money should not be a privilege.
On Stables, retrocession is designed to express the universal right to basic money access, through treasury-funded distribution rules.

Post 2
Retrocession is not a lottery and not a promise of returns.
It is a rule-bound program: eligibility is computed from verifiable economic activity inside Stables, and distributions follow transparent caps.
#Minima #Stables #BeYourBank #stablecoin

Post 3
Privacy matters. No identity cards are required.
Stables keeps participation pseudonymous, while still enforcing accountability through cryptographic commitments and qualified receipts.
#Minima #Stables #BeYourBank #stablecoin

Post 4
How does pseudonymous eligibility work?
Participants bind activity under a participant-controlled Eligibility Commitment, so creating multiple wallets does not automatically multiply retrocession.

Post 5
What qualifies is not “account creation”.
It is qualified spend and usage tied to settlement events, supported by merchant interaction validations.

Post 6
This is identity-by-interaction, not identity-by-disclosure.

Post 7
To keep it fair, retrocession uses caps and diminishing returns.
A wealthy participant should not receive 100x more just by multiplying accounts or scaling in a way that makes outcomes meaningless for others.
