# The 16 Big Mac® Ambassador Economy - Full Scenarios

The Stables Ambassador framework is designed as a self-sustaining economic loop where everyone starts with skin-in-the-game. All transactions are priced in Big Mac® units, indexed globally to ensure fairness.

## 🏛️ Core Principles
- **Entry Floor:** 100% of the first listing fee (16 units) goes to the Council Treasury.
- **Sustainable Split:** For every subsequent onboarding, the Treasury always captures 50% (8-9 units).
- **Active Reward:** Onboarders earn 7 units for their work.
- **Mentorship Reward:** Mentors earn 1 unit for building and supporting their cells.

---

## 📋 Scenario Breakdown

### Scenario 1: The New Entry (Anchor)
Every journey starts with a direct contribution to the treasury. This prevents anyone from bypassing the system or discounting their own entry.
- **User:** A new Shop or aspiring Ambassador.
- **Action:** Lists themselves for the first time.
- **Payment:** 16 Big Mac®.
- **Distribution:** 16 Big Mac® → **Council Treasury**.
- **Result:** The user is now active in the index and holds Ambassador privileges.

### Scenario 2: Direct Onboarding (The Pitch)
When an established Ambassador performs a sales pitch and onboards a new merchant.
- **User:** Ambassador A (Mentor) onboards Merchant B.
- **Action:** Merchant B lists using A as their referrer.
- **Payment:** 16 Big Mac® (from B).
- **Distribution:** 
    - 8 Big Mac® → **Council Treasury**.
    - 8 Big Mac® → **Ambassador A**.
- **Result:** Ambassador A is rewarded for the pitch and onboarding effort.

### Scenario 3: Hub Expansion (The Mentorship)
This is where the network scales. Ambassador A is now rewarded for having mentored Merchant B into an active onboarder.
- **User:** Merchant B (onboarded by A) onboards Shop C.
- **Action:** Shop C lists using B as their direct referrer.
- **Payment:** 16 Big Mac® (from C).
- **Distribution:**
    - 8 Big Mac® → **Council Treasury**.
    - 7 Big Mac® → **Merchant B (Active)**.
    - 1 Big Mac® → **Ambassador A (Mentor)**.
- **Result:** B earns for the active work; A earns a passive reward for providing the root mentorship and support.

### Scenario 4: The Independent Onboarding (Solo Path)
If an Ambassador chooses to work without a mentor (self-onboarded), the treasury acts as the default support system.
- **User:** Merchant B (self-listed) onboards Shop C.
- **Action:** Shop C lists using B as their direct referrer.
- **Payment:** 16 Big Mac® (from C).
- **Distribution:**
    - 9 Big Mac® → **Council Treasury** (Active share + Mentor share).
    - 7 Big Mac® → **Merchant B (Active)**.
- **Result:** B earns their standard reward. The extra mentorship unit reverts to the Council. There is zero financial gain for B to bypass A.

---

## 🛡️ Game Theory Outcomes
- **Zero Bypass Incentive:** By fixing the active reward at 7 units, bypassing the mentor yields no extra income for the onboarder.
- **Treasury Shield:** The Council always receives at least 50% (8 or 9 units), ensuring protocol sustainability.
- **Network Effect:** Professional Ambassadors are incentivized to build "hubs" and train their merchants, creating a viral, high-quality growth loop.
