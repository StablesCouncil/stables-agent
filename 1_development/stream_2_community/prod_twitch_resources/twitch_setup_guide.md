## 📋 Step-by-Step Setup

### Step 1: Customize Channel
1. Click **"Edit"** button on the banner notification
2. Or go to **Creator Dashboard** → **Settings** → **Channel**

### Step 2: Profile Picture
1. Click on your profile icon
2. Upload `master_symbol.png` (with white circle background)
3. Save changes

### Step 3: Banner
1. Click **"Add a banner"**
2. Upload Twitch banner (we'll create this)
3. Adjust positioning
4. Save

### Step 4: About Section
1. Scroll to **"About"** section
2. Add channel description (see template above)
3. Add social links:
   - Twitter: @StablesCouncil
   - Instagram: @StablesCouncil
   - Facebook: facebook.com/StablesCouncil
   - Website: https://minima.global (or your site when ready)

### Step 5: Panels
1. Go to **"About"** tab on your channel
2. Click **"Edit Panels"**
3. Add panels with custom images and descriptions
4. Save each panel

### Step 6: Stream Settings
1. Go to **Creator Dashboard** → **Settings** → **Stream**
2. Set stream title template
3. Set default category
4. Configure stream delay (if needed)

### Step 7: Schedule
1. Go to **Creator Dashboard** → **Settings** → **Schedule**
2. Add recurring stream times
3. Enable schedule display on channel

## ⚙️ Technical Setup

### Streaming Software:
- **OBS Studio** (Free, recommended)
- **Streamlabs** (Free, beginner-friendly)
- **XSplit** (Paid, professional)

### Stream Settings (Recommended):
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (1080p) or 1280x720 (720p)
- **Frame Rate**: 30 or 60 FPS
- **Bitrate**: 3000-6000 kbps (depending on upload speed)
- **Encoder**: x264 or NVENC (if you have NVIDIA GPU)

### Overlays:
- Keep it minimal and professional
- Use Stables colors (dark slate, cyan accents)
- Include "Built on MINIMA" badge
- Show social media handles

## 🤔 Should You Stream?

**Pros:**
- ✅ Ultimate transparency (live development)
- ✅ Real-time community engagement
- ✅ Educational content
- ✅ Builds trust through visibility
- ✅ Aligns with "Building in Public" philosophy

**Cons:**

- ❌ Requires consistent schedule
- ❌ Need decent internet connection
- ❌ Learning curve for streaming software

**Recommendation:**
Start with 1 stream per week (1-2 hours) and adjust based on:
- Your availability
- Community interest
- Content quality
