## 5. Hashtag Rules (The "Don'ts")

> [!CAUTION]
> Avoid these practices to maintain institutional credibility:

1. **No Spam Tags**: Never use generic tags like `#crypto`, `#blockchain`, `#bitcoin` unless directly relevant.
2. **No Hype Tags**: Avoid `#ToTheMoon`, `#WAGMI`, `#LFG` - inconsistent with neutral/authoritative voice.
3. **Maximum 4 Tags**: More than 4 hashtags reduces engagement and looks desperate.
4. **No Competitor Tags**: Don't tag `#USDT`, `#USDC`, `#DAI` unless making a direct comparison.
5. **No Trending Hijacking**: Don't use unrelated trending tags for visibility.
