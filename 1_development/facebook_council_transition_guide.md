## Recommended Setup: Use Business Manager

### Why Business Manager?

**Designed for Transitions**:
- ✅ Pages are owned by the Business, not a personal account
- ✅ Multiple people can be admins
- ✅ Ownership can be transferred
- ✅ Professional asset management
- ✅ Cleaner separation of personal/business

### How to Set Up Business Manager

1. **After creating your personal account and verifying**, go to:
   - https://business.facebook.com/

2. **Create Business Manager**:
   - Click "Create Account"
   - Business Name: **Stables Council**
   - Your Name: [Your name]
   - Business Email: `StablesCouncil@protonmail.com`

3. **Create the Page THROUGH Business Manager**:
   - In Business Manager, go to "Pages"
   - Click "Add" → "Create a New Page"
   - Follow the page creation steps from the main guide
   - **Important**: The page is now owned by Business Manager, not your personal account

4. **Add Other Admins** (when ready):
   - In Business Manager → "People"
   - Click "Add" → "Add People"
   - Enter their email addresses
   - Assign role: "Admin" or "Employee"
   - They can now help manage the page

## How to Transfer Ownership (When Ready)

### Option 1: Transfer Business Manager Ownership

**Steps**:
1. In Business Manager, go to "Business Settings"
2. Click "Business Info"
3. Click "Transfer Business"
4. Enter the email of the new owner (Council representative)
5. They accept the transfer
6. Ownership is transferred

**Result**: New owner has full control of Business Manager and all assets (including the Page)

### Option 2: Add Council Admins, Remove Yourself

**Steps**:
1. Add all Council members as Business Manager admins
2. Ensure they all have full access
3. Remove yourself as admin
4. Transfer ownership to a Council representative

**Result**: You no longer have access, Council has full control

### Option 3: Transfer Page Only (Not Recommended)

**Steps**:
1. In Page Settings → "Page Roles"
2. Add Council representative as Admin
3. Once they accept, you can remove yourself
4. They become the primary admin

**Limitation**: They still need a personal Facebook account, and the page isn't in Business Manager

## FAQ

**Q: Can I transfer the page without Business Manager?**  
A: Yes, but it's messier. You'd add Council members as page admins then remove yourself. Business Manager is cleaner.

**Q: Will my personal account always be tied to this?**  
A: Your personal account created it, but once you transfer Business Manager ownership and remove yourself as admin, you have no connection to the page anymore.

**Q: What if I want to remain involved after transition?**  
A: The new Business Manager owner can keep you as an admin. You'd still have management access, just not ownership.

**Q: Can the Council change the page name/username later?**  
A: Yes, whoever has admin access can change page details (within Facebook's rules).

**Q: What happens to all the content/followers during transition?**  
A: Nothing changes. All posts, followers, messages, etc. remain intact. Only the admin access changes.

**Q: Is this legally binding?**  
A: This is about Facebook account access, not legal ownership. You should have separate legal documentation for IP/trademark ownership.
