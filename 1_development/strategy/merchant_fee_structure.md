# Merchant Fee Structure & Delisting Policy

**Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: 2026-02-03  
**Status**: Development Draft

---

## 🎯 Core Principle

**Grandfather early adopters with lifetime listing, allow Council to adjust fees for new merchants, enable delisting of inactive/disappeared merchants.**

---

## 💰 Fee Structure

### **Current Policy (Launch → Year 1)**

**Lifetime Listing Fee:**
- **One-time payment:** $100 (or equivalent in Stables)
- **Benefits:**
  - Listed in merchant directory forever
  - No recurring fees
  - Grandfathered status (protected)
  - Early adopter recognition

**Why Lifetime?**
- Rewards early adopters
- Simple and predictable
- Encourages merchant adoption
- Builds trust

---

### **Future Policy (Year 2+)**

**Annual Listing Fee:**
- **Yearly payment:** $50/year (or equivalent in Stables)
- **Benefits:**
  - Listed in merchant directory for 1 year
  - Renewable annually
  - Standard merchant status

**Why Annual?**
- Sustainable long-term model
- Ensures active merchants only
- Provides ongoing revenue for directory maintenance
- Council can adjust based on market conditions

---

## 🔒 Grandfathering Policy

### **Who Gets Grandfathered?**

**Lifetime Merchants (Protected):**
- Any merchant who paid the lifetime fee before the policy change
- Listed in Year 1 (or whenever lifetime policy ends)
- Protected from future fee changes

**Grandfathered Benefits:**
- ✅ Never pay recurring fees
- ✅ Listed forever (unless delisted for inactivity)
- ✅ Special "Founding Merchant" badge
- ✅ Featured in marketing materials
- ✅ Priority support

**Example:**
- Merchant A joins in Month 3 (Year 1) → Pays $100 lifetime → Never pays again
- Merchant B joins in Month 15 (Year 2) → Pays $50/year → Renews annually

## 🗑️ Delisting Policy

### **Grounds for Delisting**

**1. Inactivity (Automatic)**
- No transactions in 12 months
- Merchant account inactive
- No response to contact attempts

**Process:**
- Month 10: Warning email ("Your listing will expire in 60 days due to inactivity")
- Month 11: Second warning ("Your listing will expire in 30 days")
- Month 12: Delisted automatically
- Can re-apply anytime (pays current fee structure)

---

**2. Merchant Disappeared (Manual Review)**
- Business closed
- Website down
- Phone disconnected
- Physical location closed

**Process:**
- Community reports merchant as closed
- Council investigates (3 verification attempts)
- If confirmed closed: Delisted
- If uncertain: Mark as "Unverified" (not delisted yet)

---

**3. Fraud or Misconduct (Immediate)**
- Scamming customers
- Not honoring Stables payments
- Violating terms of service
- Illegal activities

**Process:**
- Community reports issue
- Council emergency review (24-48 hours)
- If confirmed: Immediate delisting
- Merchant can appeal (Council vote required to reinstate)

---

**4. Non-Payment of Annual Fee (Year 2+)**
- Applies only to non-grandfathered merchants
- Fee not paid by renewal date

**Process:**
- 30 days before renewal: Reminder email
- 7 days before renewal: Final warning
- Renewal date: Grace period (7 days)
- Day 8: Delisted automatically
- Can re-apply anytime (pays current fee + $10 reactivation)

---

## 🛡️ Protection for Grandfathered Merchants

**Grandfathered merchants can ONLY be delisted for:**
- ✅ Inactivity (12 months no transactions)
- ✅ Business closed/disappeared
- ✅ Fraud or misconduct

**Grandfathered merchants CANNOT be delisted for:**
- ❌ Non-payment (they're lifetime)
- ❌ Policy changes
- ❌ Fee increases

---

## 📊 Fee Adjustment Authority

### **What is FIXED (Immutable)**

**Grandfathered Merchant Rights:**
- Lifetime merchants never pay recurring fees
- Cannot be retroactively charged
- Protected from policy changes

**Delisting Grounds:**
- Inactivity threshold (12 months)
- Fraud/misconduct grounds
- Appeal process

---

### **What is FLEXIBLE (Council-Governed)**

**New Merchant Fees:**
- Council can adjust annual fee for new merchants
- Requires Council vote (simple majority >50%)
- 30-day notice to community
- Cannot affect grandfathered merchants

**Fee Adjustment Bounds:**
- Minimum: $25/year (prevents spam)
- Maximum: $200/year (prevents gatekeeping)
- Council can adjust within these bounds

**Examples:**
- Year 2: Council sets fee at $50/year
- Year 3: Council increases to $75/year (more merchants, higher demand)
- Year 4: Council decreases to $40/year (encourage adoption in new regions)

---

**Delisting Process Refinements:**
- Council can adjust inactivity threshold (6-24 months)
- Council can adjust verification process
- Council can adjust appeal procedures

## 🔄 Reactivation Policy

### **Delisted Merchant Wants to Return**

**Scenario 1: Delisted for Inactivity**
- Can reactivate anytime
- Pays current fee structure (annual or lifetime if still available)
- No penalty
- Previous transaction history preserved

**Scenario 2: Delisted for Non-Payment**
- Can reactivate anytime
- Pays current annual fee + $10 reactivation fee
- No penalty beyond reactivation fee
- Previous transaction history preserved

**Scenario 3: Delisted for Fraud**
- Must appeal to Council
- Requires supermajority vote (>66%) to reinstate
- May require additional verification
- Previous fraud record noted

## 🚨 Edge Cases & Solutions

### **Edge Case 1: Merchant Closes, Then Reopens**
- **Solution:** Can reactivate anytime, pays current fee, no penalty. Previous history preserved.

### **Edge Case 2: Merchant Has No Transactions (New Business)**
- **Solution:** Inactivity clock starts from listing date, not first transaction. New merchants have 12 months to get first transaction.

### **Edge Case 3: Merchant Disputes Delisting**
- **Solution:** Appeal to Council, Council reviews evidence, decision within 14 days, decision is final.

### **Edge Case 4: Council Wants to Change Grandfathered Terms**
- **Solution:** CANNOT. Grandfathered terms are immutable. Council can only adjust fees for NEW merchants.

### **Edge Case 5: Merchant Sells Business to New Owner**
- **Solution:** Grandfathered status transfers with the business (same location, same category). New owner inherits lifetime listing.
