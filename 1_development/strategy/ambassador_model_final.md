# Ambassador Incentive Model - Game Theory Solution

**Version**: Game Theory Final  
**Date**: 2026-02-13  
**Problem**: Incentivize ambassadors + Allow self-listing + Prevent gaming + Open positions

---

## 🎮 THE GAME THEORY PROBLEM

**Constraints:**
1. Ambassadors must be rewarded for onboarding merchants
2. Merchants must be able to self-list (no artificial friction)
3. Merchants shouldn't game the system by becoming ambassadors just to save money
4. Ambassador positions must be open (anyone can become one)
5. Must be simple and straightforward

**The Paradox:**
- If ambassador listing is cheaper → Merchants become ambassadors just to save money (gaming)
- If both cost the same → No incentive to use ambassadors (they provide no value)
- If self-listing is cheaper → No one uses ambassadors (why pay more?)

---

## 💡 THE SOLUTION: TIME-DELAYED EARNINGS

### **Pricing Structure**

**Self-Listing (Immediate):**
- Pay: 100 Stables
- Get: Listed immediately
- Cost: 100 Stables (no refund)

**Ambassador Listing (Cheaper but requires ambassador):**
- Pay: 100 Stables
- Split: 50 Council, 50 Ambassador
- Cost: 100 Stables
- Benefit: Onboarding support + community connections

**Ambassador Earnings:**
- Earn 50 Stables per merchant you list (not yourself)
- BUT: You must list at least 5 merchants to unlock earnings
- First 5 merchants: Earnings locked
- Merchant 6+: All earnings unlocked (including first 5)

---

## 🎯 WHY THIS WORKS (GAME THEORY)

### **Merchant Decision Tree:**

**Option A: Self-List**
- Cost: 100 Stables
- Time: Immediate
- Support: None
- **Rational choice if**: Want immediate listing, don't need support

**Option B: Use Ambassador**
- Cost: 100 Stables (same price)
- Time: Depends on ambassador availability
- Support: Onboarding, community, promotion
- **Rational choice if**: Value support and community connections

**Option C: Become Ambassador to List Yourself**
- Cost: 100 Stables (same as self-listing)
- Earnings: 50 Stables (but locked until you list 5 more merchants)
- Extra work: Must onboard 5 more merchants to unlock
- **Rational choice if**: Plan to onboard other merchants anyway (beneficial!)

### **Nash Equilibrium:**

**Merchants who just want to be listed:**
- Self-list for 100 (simple, immediate)
- No reason to become ambassador (same cost, more work)

**Merchants who want to grow ecosystem:**
- Become ambassadors
- List themselves + 5+ others
- Earn 6 × 50 = 300 Stables (unlocked after 6th merchant)
- **This is beneficial behavior we WANT to encourage**

**Dedicated Ambassadors:**
- List 50 merchants
- Earn 50 × 50 = 2,500 Stables (all unlocked after 5th merchant)
- Real compensation for community building

## 🛡️ PREVENTS GAMING

**Why merchants won't game the system:**

**Gaming attempt:**
- Merchant thinks: "I'll become ambassador to save money"
- Reality: Costs 100 either way (self-list or ambassador-list)
- To unlock the 50: Must list 5 MORE merchants
- **Not worth it unless actually building community**

**Result:**
- Only merchants who genuinely want to build ecosystem become ambassadors
- This is exactly the behavior we want!
- No gaming, just organic growth

## 🔧 ALTERNATIVE: AMBASSADOR BOND

**Even Simpler Solution:**

**Ambassador Requirements:**
- Deposit 500 Stables bond to become ambassador
- Bond returned after listing 10 merchants successfully
- Prevents casual gaming (serious commitment required)

**Merchant Pricing:**
- Self-list: 100 Stables (all to Council)
- Ambassador-list: 80 Stables (40 Council, 40 Ambassador)

**Why This Works:**
- Merchants save 20 by using ambassador (80 vs 100)
- But can't game by becoming ambassador (500 bond required)
- Only serious community builders become ambassadors
- Bond returned after proving commitment (10 merchants)

**Game Theory:**
- Merchant wants to save 20 Stables
- Becoming ambassador requires 500 bond
- Not rational unless listing 10+ merchants
- **Only serious ambassadors participate**
