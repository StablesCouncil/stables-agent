## Telegram vs. Other Platforms

**Use Telegram for:**
- Announcements and updates
- Long-form content (no 280 char limit)
- Reaching crypto community (very active on Telegram)
- Building international audience
- Permanent content (not 24-hour stories)

**Frequency:**
- 1-2 posts per day maximum
- Quality over quantity
- Cross-post major announcements from X
- Use Telegram for more detailed explanations
