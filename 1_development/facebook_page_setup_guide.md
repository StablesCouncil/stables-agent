## Step 2: Add Group Link to Page Description

### On Desktop:
1. Go to your **StablesCouncil** Page
2. Click **About** (left sidebar)
3. Click **Edit Page Info**
4. Find the **Description** field
5. Update it to include:
   ```
   Money that is truly yours. Secure, Pseudonymous and Unstoppable.
   
   Join our Community Group for discussions: [paste your group URL]
   ```
6. Find the **Website** field
7. You can add your group URL here: `https://facebook.com/groups/1554666465043805`
8. Click **Save Changes**

### On Mobile:
1. Go to your Page
2. Tap **Edit Page Info**
3. Scroll to **Description**
4. Add the group link in the description
5. Tap **Save**

## Step 3: Create a Page Button (Call-to-Action)

### On Desktop:
1. Go to your **StablesCouncil** Page
2. Look for **+ Add a button** below your cover photo
3. Click **+ Add a button**
4. Select **Contact You** category
5. Choose **Send Message** or **Learn More**
6. For **Learn More**:
   - Button text: "Join Community"
   - Link: `https://facebook.com/groups/1554666465043805`
7. Click **Add Button**

### On Mobile:
1. Go to your Page
2. Tap **Edit** near your profile picture
3. Tap **Add Action Button**
4. Select **Learn More**
5. Enter your group URL
6. Tap **Save**

> [!NOTE]
> Facebook may limit button options based on your Page category. If "Join Community" isn't available, use "Learn More" or "Contact Us" and link to your group.

## Step 4: Link Groups in Page Settings

### On Desktop:
1. Go to your **StablesCouncil** Page
2. Click **Settings** (left sidebar - you may need admin access)
3. Click **Linked Groups** (left menu under "Page Setup")
4. Click **Link a Group**
5. Search for **Stables Community**
6. Select your group
7. Click **Link Group**
8. Confirm the connection

### What This Does:
- Shows your group in the Page's "Groups" tab
- Creates an official connection between Page and Group
- Makes it easier for Page visitors to find the Group

### On Mobile:
1. Go to your Page
2. Tap **☰** (three lines)
3. Tap **Settings**
4. Scroll to **Linked Groups**
5. Tap **Link a Group**
6. Search and select **Stables Community**
7. Tap **Link**

## Troubleshooting

**Can't find "Linked Groups"?**
- Make sure you're an admin of both the Page and Group
- Try on desktop instead of mobile
- Check if your Page category allows group linking

**Button not showing?**
- Some Page categories have limited button options
- Try changing Page category to "Community" or "Brand"
