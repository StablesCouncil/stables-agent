What is Stables?

Stables is an independent digital currency project built on Minima, a secure global network designed to give people full control over their money.

Our goal is to create a digital dollar that remains Stables and reliable, regardless of market conditions. We prioritize security and long-term resilience over speed or speculation. The system is designed to be owned and operated by its users, without banks or middle-men.

Vision

We imagine a financial system where you truly own your assets. In Stables, the rules are open, unchangeable, and apply to everyone equally. No central authority can freeze your funds or change the rules behind closed doors.

How to participate

Test first:
A public test application with test-only currency will open soon. You can use it to try out the system safely.

Break things:
We encourage you to use the app, try to break it, and tell us what you find. This helps us ensure it is safe for everyone.

Stay involved:
Feedback is always welcome and helps shape the future of the project.

Funding & Community Coordination

At later stages, the project may be supported through two clear methods:

1. Voluntary Donations:
   Accepted once public testing begins, strictly to support ongoing work.

2. Council Memberships (Limited):
   Introduced at a later stage. These act like digital seats on a board of directors.

   Proceeds will support:
   - External security audits (checking the code for safety).
   - Organizing the community.

   Council Members:
   People who hold these Memberships form a coordination group. Their role is to validate the project's direction and approve major milestones.

Security-first approach

We do not rush. We move to the next stage only when the system is proven safe. There is no automatic launch; reliability is tested under many conditions first.

Transparency & Safeguards

Expenses are disclosed transparently. If the project ever stops, any remaining funds are intended to be returned to the users.

What this is (and isn’t)

✔ A clear statement of our plan and values
✔ An invitation to test and review
✖ Not a promise, guarantee, or investment offer

Stables prioritizes patience, verification, and resilience over speed.
