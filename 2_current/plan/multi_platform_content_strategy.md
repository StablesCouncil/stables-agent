## Overview

A 6+ month coordinated content strategy across all platforms designed to educate audiences incrementally, building from foundational concepts to technical details, ultimately converting educated followers into beta testers.

**Core Principle**: Each message teaches one small thing, building on previous knowledge, adapted to each platform's audience.

## Platform Ecosystem

### Platform Roles & Audiences

**Instagram** (@StablesCouncil)
- **Audience**: General public, crypto-curious, non-technical
- **Tone**: Simple, visual, emotional, benefit-focused
- **Content Type**: Square images, carousels, short captions
- **Goal**: Awareness & broad education

**X / Twitter** (@StablesCouncil)  
- **Audience**: Crypto community, developers, tech-savvy users
- **Tone**: Informed, technical but accessible, conversational
- **Content Type**: Threads, links, polls, technical discussions
- **Goal**: Engagement & thought leadership

**Telegram Channel** (@StablesCouncil)
- **Audience**: Committed followers, technical enthusiasts
- **Tone**: Detailed, technical, insider updates
- **Content Type**: Announcements, deep dives, technical updates
- **Goal**: Deep education & community building

**Telegram Group** (Stables Community)
- **Audience**: Active community members, testers, developers
- **Tone**: Collaborative, technical, open discussion
- **Content Type**: Q&A, technical support, feature discussions
- **Goal**: Support & feedback loop

**Discord** (Future)
- **Audience**: Same as Telegram (technical community)
- **Tone**: Same as Telegram Group
- **Content Type**: Multi-channel discussions, real-time support
- **Goal**: Scalable community management

**LinkedIn** (Phase 3+)
- **Audience**: Professionals, businesses, institutional interest
- **Tone**: Professional, business-focused, impact-driven
- **Content Type**: Use cases, financial inclusion stories, team updates
- **Goal**: Professional credibility & business adoption

## Cross-Platform Coordination

### The Same Message, Five Ways

Each content piece is **adapted** for platform, NOT duplicated:

**Example: Introducing "Pillar #1: Pay"**

| Platform | Adaptation |
|----------|------------|
| **Instagram** | Beautiful visual, "Pay instantly. No banks. No delays." + emoji-rich caption |
| **X** | Thread: "1/ Let's talk about instant payments. What if you could send money as fast as a text message? Here's how Stables makes it possible..." (4-6 tweets) |
| **Telegram Channel** | Detailed post: "Pillar #1: Pay - Technical Overview" with implementation hints, 300-500 words |
| **Telegram Group** | Discussion prompt: "We just shared Pillar #1 on our channel. What payment use cases are you most excited about?" |
| **Discord** | Same as Telegram Group (when launched) |

## Content Philosophy

### The Learning Ladder
1. **WHAT** → What can users do? (The promise)
2. **HOW (Concept)** → How does it work? (The vision)
3. **HOW (Structure)** → How are we building it? (The approach)
4. **TECHNICAL** → What's under the hood? (The technology)
5. **STORIES** → Who's using it and how? (The proof)
