Hi David Tom, thank you for being ready to build with us. We have finalized the Ambassador framework in the app.

The core objective is to reward both the active onboarding effort and the mentorship that builds the network.

Every Merchant and Ambassador pays the standard 16 Big Mac® (paid in MINIMA) to enter the system. This 100% entry fee anchors the treasury from the start.

Once you are active, the reward loop for onboarding others works through a 7/1/8 split:
- 7 Big Mac® goes directly to the Active Onboarder.
- 1 Big Mac® goes to the Mentor (the person who onboarded the Ambassador).
- 8 Big Mac® (50%) always goes to the Council treasury.

This "Fixed Active Reward" ensures that everyone is rewarded for their work while protecting the original Sales Pitcher. There is zero financial incentive for a merchant to bypass their mentor, as the reward for the worker stays the same.

This structure encourages us to build "cells" and support each other as we scale the network. It turns competition into collaboration, ruled by code.

You can find the full details on the Ambassadors page in the app, and we've documented the exact economic scenarios in the community topic.
